# Phase B: counterfactual action evaluation

Generated by `python -m lanegame.experiments.rl.diagnostics counterfactual` (13.7 min). For each sampled reachable state, every legal first action of the focal player (and a few short plans) is committed in 120 paired rollouts (common random numbers: same chance, focal and opponent streams for every candidate; the opponent's branch-turn action is identical across candidates); afterwards both seats follow the SAME learned policy (it is the continuation policy and the fixed opponent). `Q` = mean focal return from the branch turn to the end of the game. `vs pi` = advantage over the policy's own mixture Σ_a π(a)·Q(a) at that state (positive = a one-step improvement over the policy against itself). Across states, the mean is over states (the unit of replication; its 95% interval includes the Monte Carlo noise); `+sig` / `-sig` = share of states whose own paired difference exceeds ±1.96 SE. These are Monte Carlo estimates for these continuation policies, not game-theoretic values.
